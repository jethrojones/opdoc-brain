# Volunteer Onboarding

## Outcome

A first-time volunteer knows where to arrive, what to set up, who to ask, and what “done” looks like.

## Draft arrival flow

1. Arrive 30 minutes early.
2. Find the check-in table and name tags.
3. Confirm the room layout with the host.
4. Welcome participants and direct them to the activity table.
5. Leave a short note in the daily record about what worked or needs attention.
